Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

At Nevear Games, we take your privacy seriously. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website or interact with our games.

  • Game Progress & Virtual Goods: Information such as earned in-game currency (coins), unlocked skins, levels reached, and purchased items are stored to maintain your game state.
  • Cloud Saves & Authentication: We integrate Google Play Games Services to securely back up your game progress to the cloud. This links your game state to your anonymous Google Play Games Player ID so your progress is preserved across devices or upon reinstalling the app.

We also utilize third-party SDKs for advertising and gameplay analytics to maintain and optimize our services.

Data Collection

Nevear Games does not operate private backend servers or sell your personal information. However, to enhance your gameplay experience, our games collect and process certain gameplay data using third-party services:

Third-Party Services

Our games integrate third-party services for cloud saves, advertisements, and analytics. These providers may collect unique identifiers, device information, and interaction data governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • Cloud Saves & Game Services - Google Play Services
  • Advertisement - Yodo1
  • Analytics - Unity

Your Rights

Your Rights & Data Management

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under the GDPR), you have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, rectify, or request deletion of data linked to your identity.

  • Managing Cloud Save Data: Because your game save data is stored via Google Play Games Services on your connected Google account, you can manage or delete your stored data directly via the Google Play Games mobile app or under your Google Account's connected apps settings.
  • Analytics & Ads: You can reset or limit your device's Advertising ID via your Android system settings to restrict personalized tracking.
  • Inquiries: If you wish to request clarification, exercise your rights, or submit an inquiry, you can contact us at neveargames@gmail.com.

Age Certification

In order to access or use our Services, it is necessary for the user to be of legal age in each jurisdiction. At a minimum, you must be over 13 years of age, as indicated in Regulation (EU) 679/2016. However, by way of example, there are countries that raise that age of majority, such as Germany (16 years), France (15 years) and Italy (14 years). The user guarantees and responds to the fulfillment of this requirement.

Updates to Privacy Policy

Nevear Games reserves the right to modify or discontinue, temporarily or permanently, any part of our services with or without notice.
