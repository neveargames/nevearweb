About Nevear Games

Nevear Games is an independent studio born from a raw passion for game development and a deep love for the horror genre. While our journey started with a focus on psychological thrillers, we love exploring diverse game genres from deep atmospheric horror to engaging casual 2D action games.

Whether we are crafting slow-burn mysteries that linger long after the screen goes dark or fast-paced casual titles, we pour our heart into creating gameplay experiences that resonate. Every project we build starts with a dedication to creativity, fun, and the craft of game design.
